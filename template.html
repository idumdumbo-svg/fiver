How the maths works

Rounding. Every spend rounds up to the next $5. Spend $14, log $15. The $1 difference is yours to keep.

Fixed costs. Rent and bills count toward the day's total but sit outside the comparison — otherwise rent week would look like a spending disaster.

Forgotten days. A day you never opened is skipped, not counted as $0. Only days with entries or a tapped no-spend day feed the average.

The sweep. Round-up change and the under-baseline bonus are tracked separately, each can only be moved once, and every move records where it landed.

Money you have. One number for everything you own — accounts, savings, cash in your pocket. You read it off your bank once, then every spend and every payday moves it. Spending moves it by what you actually paid, not the rounded figure, so it stays honest. Sweeps don't move it at all: parking money in a savings account doesn't make you richer.

Income. Logged exactly, never rounded. It sits outside the day total, the blocks and the baseline — those are about spending only. It shows up in In vs out.
